Teaching

Teaching software engineering, grounded in how it's practiced.

I teach across the undergraduate curriculum in the Department of Computer Science at Omar Al-Mukhtar University, drawing on empirical software engineering research to connect course material to how software is actually built and maintained.

Teaching philosophy

Software engineering is learned by building, breaking, and rebuilding systems under realistic constraints. My courses are structured around hands-on projects and code review as a graded activity. I try to bring findings from my own empirical research — on how developers actually use tools, repositories, and crowd resources — directly into the classroom, so students see the discipline as it's practiced today, not as a fixed set of rules.
